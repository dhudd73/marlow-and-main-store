How We Choose

The whole point of Marlow & Main is trust, so here are the rules — in plain sight, where you can hold us to them.

1. Hands on, or labeled

Every pick is labeled Tested (we bought it and used it) or Researched (we studied ingredients, manuals, and owner reports). We never dress research up as testing.

2. Commissions never buy placement

A higher payout does not move a product up the page. If the better product pays less, the better product still wins.

3. Show the work

Reviews state how we tested, what we measured, and where the product fell short — including a "who should skip this" section on every review.

4. Honest labels

Sample and in-progress picks say so. No invented prices, ratings, or results — prices live at the merchant and change, so we link you there to check.

5. Readers stock the shelves

When the same product or problem comes up again and again in the Suggestion Box, it moves to the front of our review line.

6. Corrections in the open

If we get something wrong, the review gets an update log entry — dated, visible, and never quietly rewritten.
